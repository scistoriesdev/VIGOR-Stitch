Scientific Premise

What VIGOR is investigating

The Vitiligo Genetics of Onset and Relapse (VIGOR) Study is a five-year longitudinal research study federally funded to understand the biological mechanisms of vitiligo. VIGOR studies changes in skin and blood, along with genetic and environmental factors, to identify signs associated with the onset and progression of vitiligo.

Researchers hope to identify patterns that may help predict the onset or worsening of vitiligo. The study aims to identify early changes associated with the onset and progression of vitiligo.

“Unlocking the secrets of vitiligo.”

Who Can Join?

Individuals age 6 or older in the United States diagnosed with vitiligo, as well as biological family members who do not have vitiligo.

Family members without vitiligo help researchers study the genetic and environmental factors associated with the condition.

Participant Information

Common Questions

Essential facts about voluntary participation, privacy safeguards, and study logistics.

VIGOR (Vitiligo Genetics of Onset and Relapse) is a five-year longitudinal research study designed to identify factors associated with the onset and progression of vitiligo. It is an observational study, not a clinical trial testing treatments.

There are no costs to participate in the study. All study collection kits and return shipping materials are provided to participants.

No. The VIGOR study is conducted entirely remotely. All surveys are completed online and all biological sample collections take place safely in your home with no clinic or hospital visits required.

Your samples and information will be handled according to the study’s approved research and privacy procedures.

Yes. Individuals age 6 or older residing in the United States can participate in VIGOR, with parental or guardian consent and child assent.

Participants should be motivated to participate for the full five-year study duration. Participation is voluntary, and participants may withdraw at any time according to approved study consent guidelines.

Have protocol questions not answered here? Inquire directly with the study coordinators at vigor@umassmed.edu or telephone our dedicated participant helpline.
