Scientific Premise

What VIGOR is investigating

The Vitiligo Genomic & Observational Research (VIGOR) initiative is a five-year longitudinal cohort study funded by the National Institute of Arthritis and Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases (NIAMS). Led jointly by Dr. John Harris at UMass Chan Medical School and Dr. Manuel Garber in computational biology, the investigation uses high-frequency home sample collection to capture the earliest immune shifts that trigger melanocyte detachment and pigment loss.

By analyzing epigenetic marks, circulating skin-homing T cells, and cell-free DNA at regular intervals in real-time, the research team aims to build the first clinical predictive algorithm capable of forecasting disease onset, flare, and spontaneous repigmentation before visible macules appear.

“Unlocking the secrets of vitiligo.”

Who Can Join?

Participation is open to individuals in the United States and Canada aged 6 and older who have been diagnosed with vitiligo, as well as their biological first-degree and extended family members (parents, biological siblings, children, aunts, uncles, and cousins) who do not have vitiligo.

The study needs more participants without vitiligo than with; family controls are crucial to isolating genetic switches.

  • check_circleProband participants diagnosed with non-segmental or segmental vitiligo.
  • check_circleBiological unaffected relatives serving as vital paired genomic controls.
  • check_circleResiding in the continental United States, Alaska, Hawaii, or Canada.
Eligible age span: 6 to 85 years at time of baseline enrollment.

What’s Involved?

The entire protocol takes place safely in your home environment. Once enrolled through the confidential digital study portal, participants follow a structured seasonal schedule requiring approximately 30 minutes per interval.

1

Baseline Genetic Assent & Saliva Tube

One-time initial DNA collection tube shipped to your residence with prepaid laboratory return packaging.

2

Quarterly Digital Health Check-In (Every 3 Months)

Secure 10-minute web questionnaire tracking life stress, medication updates, diet changes, and photographic macule review.

3

Mailed At-Home Sample Collection (Every 6 Months)

Painless gentle dermal adhesive strips and finger-prick micro-samplers safely administered without phlebotomy clinic appointments.

Strict 5-year duration; zero hospital visits or travel required at any point.

Who We Are

The VIGOR leadership brings together globally recognized leaders in autoimmune melanocyte biology, deep computational bioinformatics, statistical genomics, and compassionate clinical dermatology. Our investigators hold joint appointments across UMass Chan Medical School and Mass General Brigham Harvard Medical School, uniting bedside clinical discernment with cutting-edge molecular sequencing.

Institutional Alliance

Joint Collaboration & Specimen Infrastructure

The VIGOR study combines the specialized clinical care and translational dermatology leadership of UMass Chan Medical School with the expansive biomedical network and genomics infrastructure of Mass General Brigham. Every mailed biospecimen is processed through standardized chain-of-custody protocols inside the Worcester Molecular Core, maintaining federal research protection standards under 42 U.S.C. 241(d).

By harmonizing robotic biobank cold-storage with deep single-cell RNA sequencing, VIGOR provides our global scientific community with an unprecedented, high-resolution atlas of human vitiligo progression over five unbroken years.

Common Questions

Participation is 100% free of charge. All test kits, skin tape samplers, saliva collection tubes, instructions, and pre-addressed trackable courier shipping labels are provided to you without any out-of-pocket cost or health insurance billing.

All specimens are de-identified with an encrypted alphanumeric barcode before laboratory processing. By federal law under Section 301(d) of the Public Health Service Act, research data is protected by a Certificate of Confidentiality, preventing disclosure to insurers, employers, or legal subpoenas.
